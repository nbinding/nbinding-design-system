Appearance

Rules every component follows

  • Works at 320px wide with no sideways scrolling, at 150% text and with OpenDyslexic. The tests check that the browser never widens the layout.
  • Every state has a shape, not just a colour. Text meets WCAG AA contrast in light and dark.
  • Tap targets are at least 44 by 44px; primary buttons are 60px tall.
  • Real buttons, links, inputs and labels. Icon-only buttons have an aria-label; decorative SVGs are aria-hidden.
  • Sheets and dialogs trap focus, close with Escape and give focus back. Holding is never the only way to do something: there is always a button.
  • The sticky dock never hides the focused element (scroll padding, WCAG 2.4.11).
  • Reduce motion and the operating system's reduced-motion setting turn off transitions.
