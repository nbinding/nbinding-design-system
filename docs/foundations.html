Foundations

Colour, type, space, shape and the hatch patterns. Everything else is built from these tokens.

Colour

Dark grey and white only. Each token has a light and a dark value; text pairs meet WCAG AA (checked by the tests).

TokenUse
--nb-surfacePage background
--nb-inkText, outlines, hatching, solid states
--nb-mutedSecondary text (4.5:1 or better on surface and panel)
--nb-faintPast days and placeholders only (decorative or disabled)
--nb-lineDividers
--nb-panelDay squares, fields, chips, the rare card
--nb-accent / --nb-accent-inkPrimary buttons and solid states, and the text on them
--nb-ringFocus outline
--nb-errorErrors, always with words

Type

Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, dates and numbers; Inter for everything else. Both are self-hosted.

Hatching

The signature. Use --nb-hatch-a (45°) for one party and --nb-hatch-b (−45°) for the other. Where both apply, go solid.
